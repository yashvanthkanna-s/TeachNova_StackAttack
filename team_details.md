**Domain:** Cloud Computing & DevOps  

---

## Problem Statement
Engineering teams lack a unified, real-time view of the health of their GitHub repositories. Without deep observability into their development lifecycle, they cannot proactively detect stagnating codebases, over-reliance on a single developer, PR review bottlenecks, or volatile code changes. Currently, there is no secure, cloud-integrated tool that aggregates these signals and persists historical trend data for long-term analysis.
